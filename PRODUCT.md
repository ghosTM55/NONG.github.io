# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors, cultural organizations, rights holders, and prospective creative partners evaluating NONG Studio's point of view and fields of practice.

## Product Purpose

Present NONG Studio through a single-screen landing page, then guide visitors into a visual Index covering Intro, Curation, Partnerships, Originals, and Contact. Intro is a separate overview of IP Development, Digitalization and Activation. Curation is reserved for the formal exhibition content; the existing interactive prototypes live under Demo, accessed by entering `/demo/` directly, with no public navigation entry on desktop or mobile. This hides discovery, not access; navigation inside Demo remains available. The site communicates the studio's work across culture, entertainment, and technology without inventing unsupported clients, cases, claims, or slogans.

## Brand Personality

Cultured, precise, experimental. The experience should feel art-directed and confident, with a controlled sense of motion rather than decorative spectacle.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, conventional pill-shaped calls to action, architectural imagery unrelated to the studio's work, crude particle demonstrations, excessive full-screen decoration, and interactions that move text or compromise reading.

## Design Principles

1. Let one clear idea dominate each viewport.
2. Use imagery and motion to clarify meaning, not to fill space.
3. Preserve NONG Studio's strict typographic and spatial alignment while allowing selective visual surprise.
4. Make interaction states unmistakable without moving labels or disrupting reading.
5. Keep the static Astro delivery lightweight, responsive, and production-ready.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard operability, visible focus states, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, mobile-friendly layouts, and a complete static experience when reduced motion is preferred.

## Current Production Scope

Work in English only until the content and design are approved. Chinese localization is deferred to a final, coordinated pass. Keep the language switcher hidden during this phase. Intro owns the explanation of NONG Studio's three core business practices: IP Development, IP Digitalization and IP Activation. Each can stand alone or connect within a project. Use concise copy and illustrative moving images; these visuals are not completed-project claims. Partnerships does not repeat the three practices in a separate section. Its character feature leads directly into “Imagine the possibilities”.

Contact offers a visible, copyable `contact@nong.studio` address and four optional enquiry guides for Development, Digitalization, Activation and Strategic partnerships. Each guide opens a prefilled email to the same address. Links to an enquiry's fragment reveal that guide. Keep the direct route available for early ideas and cross-practice enquiries, with no required form or invented response-time promises. Reuse the shared page title, background and chapter navigation.

Use the full name NONG Studio in all public copy. Partnerships presents immersive experience capabilities and AI character interaction under “Our strategic partners”, with individual companies named only in discreet source credits. Connect these capabilities to Development, Digitalization and Activation, keeping NONG Studio's role clear. Partner projects are not NONG Studio portfolio claims; illustrative collaborations and roadmap concepts must remain distinguishable from completed work. “Imagine the possibilities” is a vision for prospective strategic partners: stories across cultures, places that shape cities and worlds remembered across generations. Tell the larger creative story; do not explain existing partners' division of work or describe a specific implementation. Pair the invitation with evocative, open-ended artwork, using an unfolding scroll and a distant horizon rather than devices or exhibition demonstrations. Both vision and partner invitation lead to `/contact/#partnerships`. Reuse the shared title, Curation flow background and chapter navigation. The character section currently uses a static RealNPC concept in place of the product demo video.

## Chapter Navigation

Content pages use the shared `ChapterNavigation` footer: Landing on the left, Explore Index at the exact center, and the next available destination on the right. Only label a destination as the next chapter when it is a published chapter; prototype links carry their own context. Keep the landing page and full-screen demos in their dedicated layouts, using `NavigationMark` for the same directional arrows and unfolding Index motif. Labels remain stationary, keyboard focus is immediate, and reduced-motion navigation stays fully visible.
