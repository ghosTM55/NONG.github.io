# NONG Studio Index — First-frame prompts, round 01

## Shared art direction

- Use case: `ads-marketing`
- Asset type: vertical website Index panel and future image-to-video first frame
- One continuous visual world across all five images: a contemporary culture-and-entertainment production space captured at five different moments
- Premium editorial campaign photography made from real stagecraft, translucent fabric, paper, projected light and restrained metal details; tactile and human, not glossy CGI
- 9:16 portrait composition; one unmistakable action in the central 40% safe zone so the image survives a narrow desktop crop and a mobile crop
- Deep forest green, warm ivory, charcoal and restrained antique gold/brass; controlled theatrical light; fine film grain; rich but readable shadows
- No visible words, letters, numerals, logos, brand marks or watermarks
- Avoid buildings and architectural models; museums and antique-object still lifes; generic corporate teams; handshakes and puzzle pieces; audio equipment and cables; random particles, grids or decorative abstract geometry; futuristic interfaces; recognizable copyrighted characters or artworks

## 01 — INTRO

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical website Index panel and future image-to-video first frame

Lighting/mood: controlled warm stage light cutting through deep green shadow; cultured, anticipatory, confident, not mysterious for its own sake
Color palette: deep forest green, warm ivory, charcoal, restrained antique gold
Materials/textures: translucent scrim, uncoated paper, natural textile, brushed brass, subtle real film grain
Constraints: the image must read as a real culture-and-entertainment production moment; no readable text, no logos, no watermark; suitable as a compelling static poster before any motion begins
Avoid: buildings, architectural maquettes, artifact displays, cluttered mood boards, random geometry, particles, sci-fi UI, neon cyberpunk, fantasy creature, audio gear
```
