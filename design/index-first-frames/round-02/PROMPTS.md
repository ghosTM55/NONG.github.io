# NONG Studio Index — First-frame prompts, round 02

This round deliberately treats the sections as different capabilities rather than chapters of one visual story. Consistency comes only from production quality, portrait cropping, restrained NONG colors and an editorial point of view.

## 01 — INTRO

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: first-frame poster for the INTRO panel of NONG Studio's website Index
Primary request: present a concise overview of a multidisciplinary culture-and-entertainment studio and make its combined capabilities immediately understandable: exhibition and experience design, film/content production, and digital creative work
Scene/backdrop: a real contemporary creative production studio during an active review, not an office portrait; one large working area contains three clearly readable production zones rather than a pile of props
Subject: one creative director stands at the center coordinating a compact team; nearby are a physical exhibition-layout prototype at human working scale, a professional cinema camera preparing a shot, and a large translucent projection surface showing a non-specific interactive visual; the three disciplines are visibly being directed as one studio practice
Style/medium: photorealistic premium editorial campaign photography, candid working moment, cultured and contemporary, tactile real-world production detail, subtle medium-format film grain
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait, slightly elevated medium-wide view, creative director and the junction of the three working zones within the central 40% safe area, strong silhouette when cropped to a narrow navigation panel
Lighting/mood: controlled studio work light with soft deep-green shadows and restrained warm-gold highlights; intelligent, active, confident
Color palette: charcoal, deep forest green, warm neutral white, restrained antique gold
Constraints: show capability through a believable working situation; no readable text on screens or papers; no logos, client names, recognizable artwork or watermark
Avoid: posed team photo, smiling at camera, generic agency office, cluttered mood board, futuristic sci-fi interface, architectural glamour shot, random abstract patterns
```

## 02 — CURATION

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: first-frame poster for the CURATION panel of NONG Studio's website Index
Primary request: communicate the ability to curate exhibitions ranging from museum projects to large culture-and-innovation events comparable in scale and energy to an international festival
Scene/backdrop: a large contemporary exhibition hall in operation, combining an immersive exhibition route, large-format media surfaces, object/display zones and a compact live-program stage; the event is active and populated, not an empty architectural interior
Subject: visitors flow through a deliberately sequenced exhibition environment while a curator or producer observes the overall experience from a slightly elevated foreground position; the central installation combines translucent media, physical displays and live audience activity without depicting any specific real client or exhibition
Style/medium: photorealistic cultural-event editorial photography, ambitious spatial art direction, believable crowd behavior and production detail, cinematic documentary realism
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait, elevated diagonal view with one dominant illuminated installation centered in the narrow safe area; foreground, middle-ground and crowd depth remain legible in a slim Index panel
Lighting/mood: immersive but readable; deep greens and warm whites punctuated by restrained gold light; a sense of discovery, public energy and scale
Materials/textures: projection scrims, modular display surfaces, stage light, printed matter without legible words, real flooring and human texture
Constraints: the image must unmistakably read as an exhibition or cultural event; no readable signage, no logos, no client names, no identifiable museum objects, no watermark
Avoid: empty gallery, pure architecture photography, trade-show booths, corporate conference, nightclub, generic LED tunnel, floating abstract geometry
```

## 03 — PARTNERSHIPS

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: first-frame poster for the PARTNERSHIPS panel of NONG Studio's website Index
Primary request: communicate strategic partnership as two different organizations contributing distinct expertise to build one substantial cultural-entertainment outcome together
Scene/backdrop: a real production floor during the installation of a large translucent media artwork, with two visibly distinct small teams working from opposite sides of the same structure
Subject: at the center, two lead collaborators jointly align and lock the final two sections of the media installation into place while their teams support the same action; the shared result becomes visibly illuminated only at the precise point of alignment
Style/medium: photorealistic premium editorial documentary photography, real people and physical production, sophisticated rather than corporate, candid concentration rather than staged celebration
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait, balanced frontal composition, collaborators and joining point centered inside the central 40% safe area; enough context to read as a meaningful co-production, not just two hands
Lighting/mood: purposeful, optimistic and precise; neutral work light with a restrained warm-gold glow at the successful connection
Color palette: deep neutral green, warm white, charcoal, restrained antique gold
Constraints: cooperation must be visible through shared work and complementary roles; no readable text, logos, flags, client branding or watermark
Avoid: handshake, puzzle pieces, contract signing, boardroom, posed group portrait, networking event, glowing network nodes, generic merger symbolism, sci-fi machinery
```

## 04 — ORIGINALS

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: first-frame poster for the ORIGINALS panel of NONG Studio's website Index
Primary request: communicate that NONG Studio develops and produces its own original film and screen works
Scene/backdrop: a real cinematic production set during a take, showing both the filmmaking apparatus and an original dramatic scene without resembling an existing film or franchise
Subject: a professional cinema camera and focus puller occupy the near foreground; beyond the lens, a director watches an anonymous actor perform inside a tactile practical set while controlled light, haze and production crew make the act of filming unmistakable; no clapperboard text
Style/medium: photorealistic high-end behind-the-scenes film photography, cinematic but honest about the production process, contemporary independent-film sensibility, subtle 35mm grain
Composition/framing: 9:16 portrait, camera body and lens create a strong foreground frame while the actor and lit scene remain centered in the central safe area; clear depth and a decisive visual hierarchy in a narrow Index panel
Lighting/mood: dramatic motivated film lighting, deep green-black shadows, warm practical light and restrained gold highlights; inventive, serious and alive
Materials/textures: professional cinema camera, matte box, focus gear, practical set surfaces, fabric costume, atmospheric haze, real skin and film grain
Constraints: clearly depict original filmmaking and production; no existing characters, famous faces, recognizable film scenes, readable text, brand marks, logos or watermark
Avoid: red carpet, cinema audience, movie poster layout, generic streaming interface, superhero or fantasy franchise imagery, glossy CGI, empty camera product shot
```
